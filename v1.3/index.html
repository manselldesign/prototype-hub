Prototype v1.3

Version 1.3 of the navigation prototypes, shown on the Indiana Zooniversity sample site only. These pages are for review and discussion. They are not live university websites.

What changed from v1.2

Both prototypes share everything outside the menu itself.

  • Menu button in the header. A small chevron button next to each main section opens its menu. The label still links to the page. Why: you can see what is in a section without leaving the page.
  • One look for the current page. Crimson, underlined and bold. Hover is crimson and underlined only. Why: a hovered link no longer looks like the page you are on.
  • Header section. Crimson, underlined and bold on the section’s own page. Bold only on the pages under it. Why: it shows which section you are in without repeating the side menu.
  • Wider side menu. 280px, up from 260px. Why: fewer page names wrap.
  • Back link on one line. A long name is cut at a whole word, with a small ellipsis button that shows the rest. Why: the menu below it stays in the same place on every page.
  • Chevrons at the end of the row. We explored chevrons on the left. They pushed some labels in, so rows without a chevron looked like sub-items of the row above. At the end of the row, every label lines up.
  • No breadcrumbs at the top of tier 1 pages. Breadcrumbs start one level down. Why: on a tier 1 page they only repeated the section name, which the header and the side menu already show.
  • Simpler breadcrumbs. No raised card behind them.
  • Icons and spacing. Rivet icons at their true 12px or 16px size, and all spacing on a 4px grid. Why: sharper icons and cleaner alignment.

A6 only. The drop-down is a raised card, to match the cards in B10.

B10 only. The card title, with its arrow, is the one link on each card. “Includes:” sits above the page names to show they are a list of what is inside, not links. No more than four cards in a row. Pages with nothing under them get a short card.

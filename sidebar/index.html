Prototype review

Sidebar (Options A/B)

A left rail for the current section, under a top bar that holds the lockup, search, and calls to action.

  • Two versions: the rail lists a page’s siblings, or only its children.
  • Search sits in the top bar, so sites without calls to action still use that space.
  • Calls to action now show on mobile, and the footer shows two levels of links.

Prototype review

The latest round of navigation prototypes. Each option is shown on three kinds of site: the Indiana Zooniversity sample, a simple site, and an extreme site with many sections. These pages are for review and discussion. They are not live university websites.

Nothing is listed yet. New prototypes appear here after they are published.

Visible header (Option D)

Main sections sit in the header and go straight to their page, with no drop-down.

  • Small style updates so every prototype looks alike and review stays on the navigation.
  • Calls to action now show on mobile.

Mega menu

The current header plus a drop-down that shows three levels of a section at once.

  • Split controls: the section name goes to its landing page, and a separate icon opens the menu.
  • Click to open: menus open on a click or tap, not on hover, so they don’t open by accident and work with a keyboard and screen reader.

  • Zoo site

    Indiana Zooniversity, a large sample site.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon/#/

  • Simple site

    Quick Check, a small site with few pages. Only Resources has a menu.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon-simple/#/

  • Extreme site

    School of Social Work, a site with many sections. Twelve sections wrap onto a second line.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon-extreme/#/

Sidebar (Options A/B)

A left rail for the current section, under a top bar that holds the lockup, search, and calls to action.

  • Two versions: the rail lists a page’s siblings, or only its children.
  • Search sits in the top bar, so sites without calls to action still use that space.
  • Calls to action now show on mobile, and the footer shows two levels of links.

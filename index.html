Mega menu

Indiana Zooniversity top navigation. These open a large menu on hover, by clicking the section, or by clicking the icon.

  • mega-click-icon-extreme-2deep

    School of Social Work. The menu is one column of that section’s direct children, 8px apart.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon-extreme-2deep/#/

  • mega-click-icon-extreme

    School of Social Work in this header. Twelve items wrap onto a second line. The label goes to the page, and a chevron opens the menu.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon-extreme/#/

  • mega-click-icon-simple

    Quick Check in this header. The label goes to the page. Only Resources has a chevron, and it opens How-to Videos and User Guide. The footer stays at the bottom of the page.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon-simple/#/

  • Prototype-Mega-Click-Icon

    Indiana Zooniversity top navigation. The label is a page link and the icon opens the menu. The current page stays bold while its menu is open.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click-icon/#/

  • Prototype-Mega-Click

    Indiana Zooniversity top navigation. Desktop sections open on click, with an Overview link back to that section.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-click/#/

  • Prototype-Mega-Hover

    Indiana Zooniversity top navigation. Desktop sections open a mega menu on hover. On a narrow screen, the same sections open in a tap drawer.

    https://manselldesign.github.io/prototype-hub/mega/mega-hover/#/
